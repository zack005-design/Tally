# Tally 0.2.7 — on-device AI, smart insights and a new look

**Development build** · Android 8.0 or later · Version code 9 · Latest release

Tally now ships as one app with its on-device AI built in. Ask questions about your spending, type several expenses in one line, see a month-end forecast and your net worth, and pick a new home-screen icon. This version installs over 0.2.6 (either edition) as an update and is signed with the same certificate, so your records stay on the phone.

## Download

Download `tally-0.2.7-debug.apk` from **Assets** at the bottom of this page. There is no separate non-AI APK any more: this one includes everything the non-AI edition had, and uses the same rules if a model cannot load.

New to Tally? Follow the [installation guide](../getting-started.md).

## What's new

### Ask Tally and smarter entry

- **Ask Tally**: a small reasoning model, trained from scratch and run on the phone, answers questions such as `how much did I spend on food this month?` or `average daily spending last month`. Tally's own code does the maths and shows how it got the answer. See [Ask Tally](../features/on-device-ai.md#ask-tally-tiny-reasoning-model).
- Type several expenses at once (`chai 20 cash, samosa 30 cash`), foreign amounts with your rate (`lunch 12 usd at 83.5 cash`) and simple sums (`coffee 40 + 25 cash`).
- Category suggestions on the phone, including a personal model that learns from your own entries.
- Search transactions in plain words: `coffee over 100 last month`.

### New screens

- **Smart insights**: month-end forecast, unusual spending alerts, repeating payments, budget suggestions and duplicate warnings. See [Smart insights](../features/spending-and-planning.md#smart-insights).
- **Net worth**: your accounts plus what friends owe you, minus what you owe, with a six-month trend. See [Net worth](../features/spending-and-planning.md#net-worth).

### A new look

- Redesigned **Summary**, **Split**, **Budgets** and **Accounts**, and a one-page keypad for adding an expense.
- **Spending** groups its actions in one list; **Tools** pages are split into short sections; your avatar on Summary opens **Settings**.
- A new app logo, and ten home-screen icons to choose from in **Settings → Appearance → App icon**. See [Choose an app icon](../features/appearance.md#choose-an-app-icon).

### Bill photos

- Reads photos in any format the phone can open (including HEIC), at full resolution, up to 40 MB, sideways or upside down, and sharpens mildly blurred photos.

## Before you update

- Export an encrypted backup first from the backup screen, and keep the file and its password.
- Install the APK over your current version. Do not uninstall first; uninstalling deletes your records.
- The home-screen icon changes to the new logo. Some launchers remove the old icon instead of replacing it; if Tally disappears from your home screen, add it again from the app list.

## Verification

GitHub Actions publishes this release only after these checks pass:

- Unit tests and Android lint, with the AI models and without them (the rule-based fallback).
- Data-safety and phone-compatibility tests on Android 8, 15, 16 and 17 emulators, including switching the app icon.
- The APK carries the existing development certificate (SHA-256 `7ddc9c94…b8b865`), the `com.smartexpense` app ID and version code 9, and requests no internet permission.

**Known limits:** the full on-device UI test suite and a physical phone have not been checked for this build. Ask Tally declines many comparisons, shares and savings plans for now. These are debug-signed development APKs, not Play Store builds. Checksums are in `tally-0.2.7-SHA256SUMS.txt` under **Assets**.
