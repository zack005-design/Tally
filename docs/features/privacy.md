# Privacy and app lock

Tally stores your finance records on your device. The Android app has no Internet permission, and its bundled receipt-recognition model runs locally.

## Accounts you maintain yourself

Wallet, GPay, and other accounts represent balances you enter and update. Tally does not access bank accounts, Google Pay activity, SMS messages, or payment credentials.

## Optional app lock

Set a PIN, pattern, or password in Android settings, then enable **Require unlock** in Tally's app-lock settings. Supported biometrics or device credentials unlock the app through Android's authentication prompt.

When app lock is enabled, Tally locks when you leave it and blocks screenshots and recent-app previews. App lock controls access to the app; it does not encrypt the database.

## Backups and exports

Automatic Android cloud backup is disabled. You choose when to export files and where to save them.

- Use a password-protected full backup when you need an encrypted, restorable copy.
- Use CSV or PDF for readable reports. These files are separate from the backup format.
- Review the contents before sharing a report with another person or service.

## Companion plugins

The Codex plugins run outside Tally. Reviewing an export requires you to attach the file explicitly in Codex. The app does not automatically send records to those plugins.
