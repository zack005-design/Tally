# Spending and planning

Understand where your money goes and plan the next month with budgets, allowances, recurring drafts, and savings goals.

<p align="center"><a href="../screenshots/all-screens/budgets.png"><img src="../screenshots/all-screens/budgets.png" width="300" alt="Monthly category budgets in Tally"></a></p>

## Review your spending

Use the calendar to explore transactions by day, then review category totals and monthly activity. Open the transactions behind a total when you need more context.

Below the chart, **Spending** groups its actions in one list: View transactions, Budgets, Smart insights, Savings goals, Recurring, Money planner, Calendar & diary, Monthly review and Year in review. **Spending details** shows or hides the monthly breakdown. Set budgets from **Budgets → Edit budgets**.

For shared bills, your personal share is distinct from the cash you paid on behalf of the group. Transfers between your own accounts stay separate from spending.

## Yearly summary

Open **Spending → Year in review** to see a whole year at once:

- total spending and income for the year
- how spending compares with last year. For the year in progress, Tally compares the same dates, so January to November is measured against January to November last year.
- spending for each month, with the highest month marked
- your top categories and their share of the year
- your spending for each [tag](everyday-money.md#tag-transactions)

Use the arrows to move between years. The summary uses the same rules as monthly reports: only your share of a shared bill counts, refunds reduce spending, and transfers and repayments are not spending. It is built only from the records you entered.

## Smart insights

Open **Tools → Money & planning → Smart insights** for analysis worked out on your phone from your own entries:

- **Month-end forecast**: what you have spent so far plus your average daily spending over the last 28 days for the days left, compared with your overall monthly budget if you set one. It appears after two weeks of records.
- **Worth a look**: a category at least 1.5 times and ₹500 above its usual level by this date (needs spending in two of the last three months), an entry of ₹1,000 or more that is at least three times your typical entry in that category over 90 days, and possible duplicates (same description and amount on the same day in the last 30 days).
- **Repeating payments**: the same description at least three times, at a steady interval from a week to a year and an amount within 10%, with the next expected date and the approximate monthly cost. Shared bills are left out.
- **Suggested budgets**: for categories without a budget this month, your middle month of the last three, rounded up to ₹100. Tap **Use** to save it as this month's budget.

When you interpret a command that matches something you already saved that day with the same amount, the draft warns you before you save a duplicate.

These are plain statistics over your records, not a trained model or financial advice. Nothing is saved or changed unless you tap **Use** or save an entry yourself.

## Net worth

Open **Tools → Money & planning → Net worth** to see what you have minus what you owe:

- **Account balances**: every account, including cash, bank and UPI.
- **Friends owe you**: outstanding shared-bill balances owed to you.
- **You owe friends**: outstanding balances you owe.

The screen lists each account and shows your account total at the end of each of the last six months, rebuilt from your saved entries. Savings goals are money set aside inside your accounts, so they are not counted twice. Loans, investments and property are not tracked in Tally, so they are not included. Nothing is changed by opening this screen.

## Plan ahead

| Tool | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly budgets | Set category limits and review recorded spending against them. |
| Budget alerts | Optionally get a local notification when this month's spending reaches 80% or 100% of a budget. Turn it on in **Reminders**. |
| Allowance planning | Plan how much money to set aside for everyday spending. |
| Recurring drafts | Prepare entries for repeated expenses. |
| Savings goals | Keep savings targets visible alongside your plans. |

## A monthly routine

1. Review the previous month's transactions and category totals.
2. Set budgets that reflect your priorities.
3. Prepare recurring drafts for regular costs.
4. Check progress throughout the month and adjust your plans when needed.

Plans and drafts help you organize records. They do not schedule bank payments or move money automatically.
