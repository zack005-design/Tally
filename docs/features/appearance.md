# Appearance

Tally has a light and a dark theme, and ten home-screen icons to choose from. By default it follows your phone's dark theme setting and uses the blue Tally icon.

## Choose a theme

1. Tap your avatar at the top of **Summary** to open **Settings**.
2. Tap **Appearance**.
3. Choose **System default**, **Light** or **Dark**. The change applies straight away and is remembered.

The status bar and navigation bar icons switch with the theme so they stay readable.

## Choose an app icon

1. Open **Settings → Appearance**.
2. Under **App icon**, tap the icon you want: **Tally** (default), **Lowercase t**, **Wallet**, **Rupee**, **Coins**, **Ledger**, **Split**, **Ask**, **Receipt** or **Classic** (the original tally marks).

Your launcher can take a few seconds to show the new icon. Some launchers remove the old icon from the home screen instead of replacing it; if Tally disappears from your home screen, add it again from the app list. Your records are not affected.

Every icon has a single-colour version for Android's themed icons (Android 13 and later).

## What stays the same

Shared receipt PDFs and exported reports always use a white page, so they print and read the same for everyone. The home-screen widget keeps its own style.

The theme and icon choices are settings on this phone. They are not part of your records or backups.
