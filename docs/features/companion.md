# Tally Companion

Use your custom Codex plugins to review a Transactions CSV exported from Tally. The Android app prepares the file; you choose whether to share it with Codex.

## Choose the plugin

| Plugin | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| [Tally Companion](../downloads.md#tally-companion) | Review exported spending, compare categories or months, and explain shared-bill cash movements. |
| [Tally Toolkit](../downloads.md#tally-toolkit) | Work on the Tally project and review user-provided expense exports. |

These are separate Codex plugin packages, not additional Android apps. Use them in Codex through its plugin setup.

## Prepare a file for review

1. In Tally, open export and choose the month.
2. Select **Transactions** and **CSV**, then export the file.
3. Review the file and explicitly attach it in Codex with your plugin available.
4. Ask a specific question, such as “Summarize my spending by category” or “Explain how repayments affected my account balance.”

For a comparison across months, attach the relevant exports and identify their periods.

## Understand the review

The review depends on the exported records you provide. Missing entries or periods limit what it can explain. Shared-bill payments, personal shares, and repayments have different meanings; use the [payment and share guide](payment-and-share.md) to understand those distinctions.

Tally does not upload your data automatically. Sharing a CSV in Codex is a separate action from storing records locally in the Android app.
